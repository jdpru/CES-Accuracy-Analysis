\item \textit{Note:} RMSE in percentage points for variables in the years in which they were used in neither matching nor CES weighting, 2008--2022. $\Delta$ equals pooled RMSE with CES weights minus pooled matching-only RMSE; negative values indicate lower error after weighting. \textcolor{gray}{$\times$} = used in matching that year; \textcolor{gray}{$\circ$} = used in CES weighting that year; \textcolor{gray}{--} = not measured or office not on the ballot. Data from 2006 are excluded because that year's matching and weighting variables are undocumented.
\item[a] Computed once across all of a variable's error observations in the years shown, not as the average of the row, so years with more observations count more.
\item[b] Arithmetic means; each variable counts equally. Year columns average the variables with an estimate in that year, so the set of variables differs across years; the Pooled column averages the pooled RMSEs. The $\Delta$ row gives each year's weighted mean minus its matching-only mean.
