## anesrake_restricted/
These are the restricted anesrake weights: raking on the four most discrepant demographics only (age group, education, family income, employment status). They were removed from Table S.1 on 2026-10-07.
- `national_anesrake_restricted_accuracy.tex` is the former bottom panel of Table S.1: national fit to the CPS targets. It is written by `figuresHQ.ipynb`, cell 86.
- `rmse_anesrake_restricted_comparable.tex` gives state-level RMSE by variable and year under the restricted weights. Its copy in `tables_and_figures/output/` is regenerated by the notebook's "Directly Comparable ANESRake Full vs. Restricted Tables" cells.
- The weights themselves are built in `code/analysisHQ.Rmd` (`anesrake_weight_restricted`). The R code still computes them.
- Use: a robustness check for a likely reviewer question, whether anesrake did poorly only because it raked on too many variables. On the down-ballot offices (US House, state senator, state representative), restricted weights gave slightly lower error than the full weights in the comparable tables (about 9.2 vs. 9.6 points, averaged over years).
