The figure summarizes the procedure for the 2008–2022 waves as documented in the CES guides. Respondents from opt-in panels are matched to a target sample drawn from an ACS-based sampling frame, and the matched sample (hatched blue) gives the matching-only estimates. Step 1 weights the matched sample to the frame using propensity score weighting (2008–2012) or entropy balancing (2014–2022), and step 2 post-stratifies it to give the final weighted sample (solid navy) and the matching + weighting estimates. Box names follow the terms table, the variables used at each stage in each year are in the stage table, and the 2006 wave, conducted by Polimetrix, followed a different procedure (Supplementary Material section E).
